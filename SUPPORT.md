# Ai_Engineering Support

Ai_Engineering provides a private, interactive curriculum for learning production AI engineering on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

For help, bug reports, subscription questions, or accessibility feedback, email lukekevinmclaughlin.oss@gmail.com or open an issue in this repository.

## Subscription help

- Start or confirm a subscription from the in-app access screen.
- Use **Restore purchases** if an active subscription is not recognized.
- Manage or cancel the subscription from Apple Account subscription settings.
- Deleting and reinstalling the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Ai_Engineering cannot access or change Apple Account billing information.
