# App Review Notes — AI Engineering

## Product purpose

AI Engineering teaches how to design and operate AI-enabled products. Its curriculum and projects focus on language-model interfaces, retrieval, agents, multimodal systems, evaluation, safety controls, inference, cost, latency, and production observability.

This is not a general machine-learning training app or a data-pipeline course. Its professional operating loop is **Design → Evaluate → Guard → Operate**.

## Suggested review walkthrough

1. Open the Dashboard and review the AI operating-loop card.
2. Open Learn and inspect courses covering retrieval, agents, evaluations, safety, inference, and AI architecture.
3. Open Projects and inspect AI-product deliverables and milestones.
4. Open Tutor Core from a lesson or project to see the AI-engineering persona and contextual grounding.
5. Confirm that free progress is preserved and purchase restoration is available from the subscription interface.

## Relationship to the engineering family

AI Engineering, ML Engineering, and Data Engineering share a reusable Swift package for visual components, learning data structures, local progress, code controls, and network safeguards. AI Engineering owns the package source.

The shared package does not contain the AI curriculum, project catalog, tutor persona, navigation state, subscription presentation, or App Store copy. Those remain specific to this product.

## Privacy and external services

Offline Tutor Core does not require an external provider. Users may optionally configure their own provider or local model server. Same-origin redirect enforcement, response-size limits, HTTPS requirements for remote hosts, and localhost-only plain HTTP are enforced by shared network safeguards.
