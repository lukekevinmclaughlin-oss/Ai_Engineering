# Ai_Engineering Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 July 2026

Ai_Engineering is designed to work privately and offline. The developer does not require an account and does not collect, sell, rent, or use personal information for advertising or tracking.

## Learning data

Course progress, XP, streaks, bookmarks, lesson answers, project workspaces, and Tutor Core conversations are stored locally on the user's device. This data is not transmitted to the developer.

## Tutor Core

Offline Core and Apple On-Device tutoring process questions and learning context locally on the user's device.

If a user explicitly enables a connected provider, the app sends the question, recent chat, and relevant course context directly to the endpoint selected by the user. Those requests are not routed through or visible to the developer. The selected provider's own terms and privacy policy apply. API credentials are stored in the device Keychain.

## Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions. Ai_Engineering receives verified entitlement information from StoreKit but does not receive payment-card details.

## Tracking and analytics

Ai_Engineering does not include advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or developer-operated analytics.
